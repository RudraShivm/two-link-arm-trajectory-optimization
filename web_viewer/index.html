Theory

Formulations

Two-link planar arm and direct transcription (Cardona-Ortiz & Arechavaleta 2025; Kelly, SIAM Review 2017).

Dynamics Two-link planar manipulator
$q=[\theta_1,\theta_2]^T$, $x=[q^T,\dot{q}^T]^T$, $\tau=[\tau_1,\tau_2]^T$.
$$M(q)\ddot{q} + C(q,\dot{q})\dot{q} + g(q) = \tau$$ $$M(q)=\begin{bmatrix} I_1+I_2+m_1 r_1^2+m_2(l_1^2+r_2^2+2l_1 r_2\cos\theta_2) & I_2+m_2(r_2^2+l_1 r_2\cos\theta_2)\\ I_2+m_2(r_2^2+l_1 r_2\cos\theta_2) & I_2+m_2 r_2^2 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$C(q,\dot{q})\dot{q}=\begin{bmatrix}-m_2 l_1 r_2\sin\theta_2(2\dot{\theta}_1\dot{\theta}_2+\dot{\theta}_2^2)\\ m_2 l_1 r_2\sin\theta_2\dot{\theta}_1^2\end{bmatrix},\quad g(q)=\begin{bmatrix}(m_1 r_1+m_2 l_1)g\cos\theta_1+m_2 r_2 g\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\\ m_2 r_2 g\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)\end{bmatrix}$$
Forward kinematics $p_{ee}=[l_1\cos\theta_1+l_2\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2),\; l_1\sin\theta_1+l_2\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)]^T$
Objective $\min\int_0^T\|\tau(t)\|_2^2\,dt$ subject to $|\tau_i|\le\tau_{\max}$
Method 1 Single shooting (RK4)
$z=[\tau_0,\ldots,\tau_{N-1}]^T\in\mathbb{R}^{2N}$. States from forward RK4; terminal constraint $x_N=x_f$.
$$x_{k+1}=x_k+\frac{h}{6}(k_1+2k_2+2k_3+k_4)$$ $$k_1=f(x_k,\tau_k),\quad k_2=f(x_k+\tfrac{h}{2}k_1,\tau_k),\quad k_3=f(x_k+\tfrac{h}{2}k_2,\tau_k),\quad k_4=f(x_k+h k_3,\tau_k)$$
Decision size $2N$ (controls only)
Jacobian Dense terminal map
Method 2 Trapezoidal collocation
$z=[x_0,\tau_0,\ldots,x_N,\tau_N]^T\in\mathbb{R}^{6(N+1)}$. Defects:
$$\zeta_k=x_{k+1}-x_k-\frac{h}{2}\big(f(x_k,\tau_k)+f(x_{k+1},\tau_{k+1})\big)=0$$ $$J=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\frac{h}{2}\big(\|\tau_k\|_2^2+\|\tau_{k+1}\|_2^2\big)$$
Decision size $6(N+1)$
Defects $4N$ equalities
Order $O(h^2)$ global
Method 3 Hermite–Simpson collocation
Nodes and midpoints. Hermite midpoint interpolant plus Simpson defect:
$$x_{k+1/2}=\frac{1}{2}(x_k+x_{k+1})+\frac{h}{8}(f_k-f_{k+1})$$ $$\zeta_k=x_{k+1}-x_k-\frac{h}{6}(f_k+4f_{k+1/2}+f_{k+1})=0$$ $$J=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\frac{h}{6}\big(\|\tau_k\|_2^2+4\|\tau_{k+1/2}\|_2^2+\|\tau_{k+1}\|_2^2\big)$$
Order $O(h^4)$ global
Midpoints Evaluated at $t_k+h/2$
